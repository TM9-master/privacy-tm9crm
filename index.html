TM9 CRM Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how [Company legal name] ("TM9", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the TM9 CRM mobile app (the "App"), package name com.crm.trademaster.

The App connects you to the broker you select with a broker code (your "Broker"). Your Broker opens and operates your trading and client accounts, and processes your deposits, withdrawals and identity verification. Your Broker also has its own privacy policy, which governs how it uses your information. Please read it as well.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Registration and sign-in: email address, mobile number, password and any partner or referral code.
  • Profile: full name, street address, city, state or province, country and an optional profile photo.
  • Identity verification (KYC): images of identity and address documents that you choose to upload.
  • Funding: amounts, cryptocurrency wallet addresses and one-time verification codes for deposits, withdrawals and transfers.
  • Trading accounts: account names, account types, leverage and trading passwords you set.
  • Support: the content of support tickets and emails you send.

Information created when you use the App

  • Account and trading data: trading account numbers, balances, equity, margin, open positions, orders, trade history, copy-trading subscriptions, price alerts and transaction history.
  • Device information: a random identifier that the App creates when it is installed, and your device platform (Android or iOS). These are sent with requests to identify your session.
  • Technical data: like any internet service, the servers you connect to receive your IP address and basic request information.

Information stored on your device

To keep you signed in, the App stores your session details on your device, including your sign-in credentials and session token, together with your selected broker and preferences such as the colour theme. Signing out removes your session details from the App.

What we do not collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, microphone audio or browsing history. It does not use your camera; photos are chosen from your gallery with the system picker. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not use your device's advertising ID.

2. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to your Broker and receive live prices.
  • Photos: only the images you pick for your profile photo or KYC documents. On older Android versions the App may request storage access for this purpose.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the App: sign you in, show your accounts, display prices and charts, and place and manage trades.
  • Open and manage trading accounts and process deposits, withdrawals and transfers through your Broker.
  • Verify your identity and meet anti-money-laundering, know-your-customer and other legal obligations.
  • Protect accounts, detect fraud and keep the service secure.
  • Respond to support requests and send service messages, such as verification codes.
  • Comply with law, regulation and requests from competent authorities.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How information is shared

  • Your Broker: account, profile, KYC, funding and trading information is sent to the Broker you select, so that it can provide its services to you.
  • Service providers: companies that host and operate the trading platform and its servers on our or your Broker's behalf, under obligations to protect the information.
  • Payment and blockchain networks: cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals are recorded on public blockchains, where wallet addresses and amounts are publicly visible.
  • Authorities: regulators, law enforcement or courts, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: a buyer or successor, if our business is reorganised, merged or sold, subject to this policy.

5. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where your Broker and our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

6. How long we keep information

We and your Broker keep information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the service. Financial regulations, including anti-money-laundering rules, may require identity, transaction and trading records to be kept for several years after an account is closed. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Security

Account, profile, KYC and funding information is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to personal information is limited to people and systems that need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your passwords private and your device protected with a screen lock.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your profile in the App at any time. To exercise other rights, contact us using the details below; we may need to verify your identity first. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account

In the App, open Profile → Security → Delete Account to send a deletion request to your Broker. You can also email [privacy contact email] from your registered email address. Close any open positions and withdraw your funds first. Your account and personal information will be deleted, except records that we or your Broker must keep by law, as described in section 6.

9. Children

The App is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App or by email.
